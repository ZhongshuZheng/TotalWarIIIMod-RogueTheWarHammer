你是否已经厌倦了每局战役动辄数十小时的经营、扩张与推图流程？
你是否无数次想开启一局新战役，却又被漫长的战役节奏劝退？

你是否觉得游戏中许多过渡兵种如同鸡肋：明明设计很有趣，却因为招募建筑独立、替代兵种更优、成型速度太慢等原因，几乎从未真正使用过？

你是否希望在《Total War: Warhammer III》中体验一种类似商队系统的 rogue-lite 玩法：快速进入战斗，持续获得奖励，不断构筑部队，并挑战越来越强的敌人？

那么，你来对地方了。

[b]Warhammer Spire | 战锤尖塔[/b] 正是这样一款为《Total War: Warhammer III》提供 rogue-lite 战役体验的 Mod。它的灵感来自《Slay the Spire》与原版震旦商队系统。

在本 Mod 中，玩家不需要进行传统的招兵、造建筑、经营城镇或大地图扩张，而是通过一连串事件与选项推进游戏流程：选择路线、构筑部队、获得随机奖励、积累战力成长，并不断挑战更加强大的敌军。

[h2]核心玩法[/h2]

[list]
[*]基于原版战役植入，但提供一套几乎独立于原战役进程的游玩体验。一切的开端，来自屏幕右下角新增的 [b]“战锤尖塔”[/b] 按钮。

[*]通过连续的决策事件参与战斗，并获得部队、英雄、神器与装备奖励。你无需进行任何传统地图经营，就可以持续推进战斗与成长。

[*]在游玩过程中，你有机会获得几乎所有种族的领主、英雄与兵种，组建属于你的超级梦之队。

[*]每轮战斗后部队会完全恢复。每场战斗都可以全力以赴，无需担心战损、补员与长期消耗。

[*]本 Mod 使用原版多人对战模式中的单位价格作为平衡基础，提供接近 30 轮的相对公平战斗体验。

[*]支持通过 MCT 调整部分难度参数。
[/list]

[h2]玩法循环[/h2]

[list=1]
[*]进入原版战役，选择任意你喜欢的种族与派系。该种族会成为你的主种族，并影响未来部队与装备奖励的基础方向。

[*]开始游戏后，点击屏幕右下角新增的两把交叠之剑按钮，启动 [b]战锤尖塔[/b] 玩法。

[*]随机生成一支事件用军队，并将其移动到合适的作战位置，开始事件链。

[*]组建部队构筑，参与战斗并取得胜利，获得单位、装备、经验与英雄成长奖励。

[*]跟随事件指引，前往不同种族节点，面对不同主题的敌军，并取得对应种族的奖励。

[*]战斗，奖励，再战斗，再奖励……直到你抵达更深层的挑战。
[/list]

[i]A Slay-the-Spire-inspired rogue-lite campaign mode for Total War: Warhammer III.[/i]

Are you tired of campaign runs that take dozens of hours of empire management, expansion, and map-painting?
Have you ever wanted to start a new campaign, only to be discouraged by how long the full campaign experience can become?

Have you ever felt that many mid-tier or transitional units are rarely worth using?
Some units have interesting designs, but because of separate recruitment buildings, better alternatives, or slow campaign progression, they often never get a real chance to shine.

Do you want a rogue-lite mode for Total War: Warhammer III that plays more like the caravan system: fast battles, frequent rewards, constant army-building, and escalating challenges?

Then you are in the right place.

[b]Warhammer Spire[/b] is a rogue-lite campaign experience for Total War: Warhammer III, inspired by [i]Slay the Spire[/i] and the original Cathay caravan system.

In this mod, you do not need to recruit units manually, construct buildings, manage settlements, or paint the campaign map. Instead, you progress through a chain of events and choices: choose your path, build your army, gain random rewards, grow stronger, and keep challenging increasingly powerful enemy armies.

[h2]Core Features[/h2]

[list]
[*]Built into the original campaign, but designed as an almost independent gameplay experience. Everything begins with the new [b]Warhammer Spire[/b] button in the lower-right corner of the campaign UI.

[*]Progress through a chain of decision events, fight battles, and earn units, heroes, artifacts, and equipment rewards. No traditional campaign management is required.

[*]During a run, you may obtain lords, heroes, and units from almost every race, allowing you to build your own dream army.

[*]Your army fully recovers after each battle. Every fight is meant to be played at full strength, without worrying about casualties, replenishment, or long-term attrition.

[*]The mod uses the original multiplayer unit prices as the basis for balance, aiming to provide a relatively fair combat experience for around 30 rounds.

[*]MCT support is included for adjusting several difficulty-related options.
[/list]

[h2]Gameplay Loop[/h2]

[list=1]
[*]Enter a normal campaign and choose any faction from any race you like. Your chosen race becomes your main race and influences the baseline of your future unit and equipment rewards.

[*]Once the campaign begins, click the new crossed-swords button in the lower-right corner of the screen to start [b]Warhammer Spire[/b].

[*]A special event army will be generated. Move it to a suitable position on the campaign map and begin the event chain.

[*]Build your army, fight battles, win rewards, gain experience, level up your heroes, and equip powerful artifacts.

[*]Follow the event chain to visit different race nodes, face themed enemy armies, and earn rewards associated with those races.

[*]Fight, earn rewards, fight again, earn more rewards... and see how deep your run can go.
[/list]

[h2]Current Version Content[/h2]

[list]
[*]Supports almost all major races and factions up to the [b]LORDS OF THE END TIMES[/b] version.

[*]Event rewards include most vanilla lords, heroes, regular units, elite units, and the majority of vanilla equipment and artifact rewards.
[/list]

[*]In the late game, especially after floor 30, there may be some stuttering before entering battle. Suggestion: consider round 30 a win and end the run.

[*]The game won't able to continue when chaos lords got replaced with daemon princes. Don't choose that.

[*]Crash may occur when playing Malakai against Ironbrow. Workaround: use a different Dwarf faction.

[*]Q: Why does my army move to a different location on the campaign map after each battle?

[*]Q: Why is there no text showing up in the event popups?

[*]Q: I can't find the mod toggle button for a certain race. What's going on?
